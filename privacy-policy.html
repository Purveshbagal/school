Privacy Policy

Little Star School Devtakli (Android app) · Effective date: 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Little Star School Devtakli student app ("the app") handles information. The app is developed by Purvesh Bagal, Infiotix Technologies ("we", "us") for Little Star School, Devtakli ("the school"), so that students and parents can view school information such as bus location, attendance, exam results, fees and notices.

1. Information we use

  • Aadhaar number (login identifier): Students sign in with the Aadhaar number that was given to the school at the time of admission. The app sends this number securely to the school's server only to verify the student and open their account. The app has no separate sign-up; accounts are created by the school administration.
  • Student academic information: After login, the app displays information that the school already holds, such as the student's name, attendance, exam marks, fee status and school notices. This information is fetched from the school's server for display only and is not stored permanently on the device.
  • Location (approximate and precise): If you allow location permission, the app uses your device location only to calculate the school bus arrival time while you are using the bus tracking feature with the app open. Location is not stored and is not shared with anyone. You can deny or turn off location permission at any time; the rest of the app will keep working.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not show advertisements in the app.
  • We do not use the Android advertising ID.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties for marketing or any other purpose.
  • We do not collect contacts, photos, files, messages or call logs. The "Quick Contact" feature only opens your phone's dialer so that you can call the school or bus driver yourself.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to provide the app's features: signing in the student, showing their school records, calculating bus arrival time and showing school announcements.

4. Children's privacy

The app is meant for students of the school, including children under 13, and their parents. Student information in the app is provided by the school as part of the admission process. We collect only what is needed to sign in and show the student's own school information, we do not show ads, and we do not share children's information with third parties. Parents or guardians can contact the school office or us (see below) to review or request correction or deletion of their child's information.

5. Data security

All communication between the app and the school's server is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Access to student records is limited to the school administration.

6. Data retention and deletion

Student records are maintained by the school as part of its normal school records. Location data used for bus arrival time is processed only in real time and is not retained. To request correction or deletion of information, please contact the school office or email us at the address below.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes (for example, if online fee payment is added). The updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
